## Indonesian Government Bonds Trend Amidst Global Uncertainty

> **Repository that Presents Indonesian Government Bonds Daily Movement, since the Beginning of COVID-19 Pandemic until September 2026.**

This repository demonstrates how to devise data visualization of high-frequency time series chart, using Indonesian Government Bonds Yield of 4 different maturity, namely 1-Year, 5-Year, 10-Year, and 30-Year. They represent the movement trend of Indonesian Bonds Market movement with daily frequency, covering January 2020 until September 2026. Moreover, The Indonesian Government 10-Year Bonds Yield is the golden indicator for gauging whether bonds market is healthy. It is become one of many economic indicators taken into consideration when formulating Macro Economic Fundamental Assumptions (also known as *Asumsi Dasar Ekonomi Makro* or ADEM in Bahasa Indonesia). Higher bonds yield indicates that Government Bonds prices are relatively inexpensive, yet it triggers increasing effect on government costs of borrowing from issuing Bonds.
